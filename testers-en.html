US Civic Test - Beta Tester Instructions

Thanks for helping test! This is a closed beta of an app for preparing for the US Citizenship Test. The app is free, in English, and made for people studying for the test.

Open the app

Beta code to enter: 1234 - enter it once, the browser remembers it.

Before you start - important

  • Use the same browser and the same device for the whole test. Your name, details, and progress are saved locally in your browser only - they do not follow you to another device.
  • Not in Incognito / private mode, and do not clear the site's data - that wipes your progress and you will have to start over.
  • On first entry, fill in once: name, test type, state, and test date. After that you never need to type them again - the app remembers.
  • If something looks broken - take a screenshot. It helps more than any description.

New in this version: daily challenge, day streak counter, share card with squad code, join-a-squad during onboarding, and ad placeholders (see below).

What is in the app - screen by screen

Home - the main screen

Here you see your stats (how many questions you answered, accuracy, day streak), a quick practice button, the daily challenge (10 new questions every day with a countdown), continuing where you left off, and links to weak spots, the Mastery Map, and your squad.

Practice Setup

Pick a test type (Citizenship 128 questions, Citizenship 100 questions, or High School), a topic and subtopic, and a session length. You can mix everything or focus on one topic.

Quiz

US civics questions with 4 answers each. After every answer you get immediate feedback with an explanation, and sometimes a picture or a memory trick. Tapping a picture enlarges it to fullscreen. You can swipe left to go to the next question. At the end of a session you reach the Review screen.

Review

Your score, correct and wrong answers, your weak spots, a share card with your squad code (screenshot it and share with friends), and a "Review my mistakes" button to retry what you missed.

Squad - the trophy tab

Create a group (Squad), get an invite code, share it with friends, and see a leaderboard. You can create several squads (for example one for family and one for friends) and switch between them. Squads sync between devices - two testers who join the same invite code will see each other on the leaderboard. You can also join a squad right from the onboarding screens.

Weak Spots

The questions you struggle with most - the app collects them automatically so you can practice just those.

Mastery Map

A map by topic showing where you are strong and where you are weak, with overall accuracy and per-topic accuracy.

Profile

Your details, statistics, and editing your test details (test type, state, date). Note: changing your state changes the answers to state-specific questions (governor, senators, capital) in your next quizzes. There is also a delete-account option that wipes all your data.

Ads - we want your opinion

In this beta you will see placeholders instead of real ads:

  • Banner at the bottom of the Home screen - how does the placement feel? Intrusive or fine?
  • Full-screen ad after each quiz - how does the timing feel? Too early, fine, or too late?

Tell us what you think about the placement and timing - it will help us tune the ad experience for the final version.

What to test

  1. A 10-question session from start to Review - do the answers and explanations make sense?
  2. Do the distractors (the wrong answers) make sense, or are there weird, unrelated options?
  3. Try the daily challenge - does the countdown work? Does it reset at midnight?
  4. Create a squad, share the code, join with another tester's code, and check the leaderboard.
  5. Try the Share button on the Review screen - does a dialog open? Is the message copied?
  6. Deliberately miss a few questions and check weak spots and "Review my mistakes".
  7. Close the browser, open it again - are your name and progress still saved?
  8. Change your state in the profile (e.g. to Texas) - do state-specific questions change accordingly?

What does NOT work in this version - do not try

  • Real ads - only placeholders are shown (see above).
  • Push notifications - they do not arrive in the web version.
  • Store download links in share messages - the app is not in the stores yet, so those links lead nowhere.
  • Payments - there is nothing to buy. The app is completely free.

How to report

Fill in the feedback form - it opens a ready-to-send email with all your answers, just press Send. Attach screenshots of bugs if you have any.
